Native dependency closure
-------------------------

SwiftSpice supplies arm64 static XCFrameworks built from pinned,
checksum-verified sources:

  libjpeg-turbo 3.2.0 ........ IJG, zlib, and BSD-3-Clause
  spice-common QUIC 0.42 ..... LGPL-2.1-or-later
  usbredir 0.15.0 ............ LGPL-2.1-or-later library components
  libusb 1.0.30 .............. LGPL-2.1-or-later

Source URLs, checksums, and the reproducible build recipe are in
the v0.1.2 `Scripts/build-native-dependencies.sh`. The precise
upstream license texts are embedded in the XCFrameworks and copied into the app.
Maspice independently verifies that the final Mach-O closure has no build-host
absolute dynamic-library paths.
